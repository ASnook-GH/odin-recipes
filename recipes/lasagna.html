Lasagna

Home

Description

A classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Basil

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef until browned.
  4. Add the tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Layer the pasta, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes until bubbly and golden.
