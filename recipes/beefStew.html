Beef & Beer Stew

Home

Description

A hearty beef stew that will keep you warm during the winter.

Ingredients

  • Bacon
  • Beef
  • Stout Beer
  • Beef stock
  • Onions
  • Carrots
  • Salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the bacon until crispy, then set aside.
  2. Cut the beef into bite-sized pieces and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Add the beef to the pan and cook until browned.
  4. Add the onions and carrots, and cook until softened.
  5. Pour in the stout beer and beef stock.
  6. Simmer for 2-3 hours until the beef is tender.
