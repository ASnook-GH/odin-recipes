Pasta Carbonara

Home

Description

Spaghetti carbonara is a lovely pasta dish that's great to serve for company. Often prepared with cream but this is the real italiant recipe

Ingredients

  • Guanciale or Bacon
  • Eggs
  • Pecorino Cheese
  • Pasta

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti in boiling water
  2. Add the Guanciale/Bacon to a skillet at a medium heat until brown
  3. Beat the eggs and mix in the pecorino cheese
  4. Add the cooked pasta to the skillet
  5. add the egg mix to the skillet and toss constantly until the eggs set.
